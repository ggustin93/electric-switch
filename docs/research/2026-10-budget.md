## Review of the budget estimate (2026-10-07)

Two independent reviews: a data scientist (calculation, run on the real `budget()`) and a car cost-of-ownership expert (assumptions vs published sources, Belgium/France).

**Confirmed:** the order of magnitude. Keeping a paid-off petrol car vs buying a new compact EV costs about **+15–18 k€ over 5 years at 12,000 km/yr**. Studies comparing new vs new (UFC-Que Choisir 2025, ICCT 2023, Test-Achats) find EV ≈ petrol, so the gap comes from the **depreciation of a new car** (~18–20 k€ on 33 k€). Its sign is robust: no single input flips it. A new EV only becomes cheaper above ~36,500 km/yr over 10 years. A 3-year-old used EV cuts the gap a lot.

**Sensitivity (default case, delta in €):** current car value 0 → 15 k€: +18.0 → +9.6 k ; km/yr 5 → 30 k: +17.7 → +8.9 k ; EV price ±20 %: +10.8 → +19.6 k ; EV depreciation 15 → 25 %/yr: +11.4 → +18.2 k ; fuel ±20 %: ±1.5 k ; electricity 0.25 → 0.45 €/kWh: ±1.2 k.
